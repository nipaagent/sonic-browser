Sonic Browser — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026 · NIPA SOLUTIONS

Sonic Browser ("Sonic") is a web browser for Android. This policy explains what information the app handles and what it does not.

We do not collect your data

Sonic has no user accounts and contains no analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs. We do not collect, store on our servers, sell or share your personal information or browsing activity. NIPA SOLUTIONS operates no servers that receive your browsing data.

Data stored on your device

Your bookmarks, browsing history, downloads list and settings are stored only on your device, so the browser can show them back to you. You can erase them at any time from the app's menu ("Clear Browsing Data") or by clearing the app's storage in Android settings. Incognito tabs do not save history on the device.

Websites you visit

When you browse, your device connects directly to the websites you visit, and those sites may collect data under their own privacy policies. Sonic's ad and tracker blocking (Shields) uses filter lists bundled inside the app; your browsing activity is not sent anywhere for blocking.

Site icons (favicons)

To display website icons in tabs, history and bookmarks, Sonic may request a site's icon from Google's public favicon service, which sends that site's domain name to Google. No other browsing information is included in these requests.

Voice search

If you use voice search, your speech is processed by your device's speech-recognition provider (for example Google), subject to that provider's privacy policy. Sonic does not record or store your voice.

Downloads

Files you download are fetched directly from the website to your device using Android's DownloadManager and are stored on your device.

Children

Sonic is a general-purpose browser and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
